How Grind District works

Grind District is a free 3D city you play in your browser. You learn Web3 by walking around and doing things, and every short lesson you pass is written onto your CV with a certificate you can share. No download, and no wallet needed to start.

The loop

Walk the city → finish a short lesson → it goes on your CV → claim a certificate (mint it on-chain if you want)

Start in 5 steps

  1. Open the game and sign up

    Go to www.grinddistrict.app on your phone or computer. Tap Sign up, pick a name and a look. Signing in with Google, X, email or NEAR keeps your progress on every device.

  2. Follow Zahra to Campus

    Your guide, Zahra, walks you to Campus and teaches the first lesson in about a minute. The Next step card at the top of the screen always shows where to go next: tap it and you walk there.

  3. Pass your first lesson

    Each lesson is a few short cards and a quick check. Pass it and it is written onto your CV with a certificate, and you earn in-game cash to spend in the city.

  4. Explore the city

    Work a paid shift at McGrind, read today's market board at Grind Café, practise trading with in-game cash, learn how crypto institutions and ETFs work at Grind Bank, and join events at the Conference centre.

  5. Share your proof

    Open Profile and tap Share my CV, or open any certificate and share its link. If you want it on-chain, take the short certificate exam and mint it as a soulbound NFT on Base or NEAR.

Moving around

  • Phone: drag the round stick at the bottom right to walk; drag anywhere else to look around. When you reach a place, a button appears: tap it to go in.
  • Computer: walk with the arrow keys or W A S D, look around with the mouse.
  • Lost? Open the menu and choose the city map: tap any place and you are there.

What is in the city

  • Campus: 42 short lessons in 6 worlds, from how the internet and money work, to spotting scams, blockchain basics, layers and real uses, the ways people earn in Web3, and your career.
  • McGrind: a burger and boba restaurant where you work paid shifts in the kitchen.
  • Grind Café: today's market talk in plain words, and what each line means for you.
  • Trading floor: practise spot trading, perpetuals and DeFi with in-game cash only.
  • Grind Bank: how crypto institutions keep your coins, spot crypto ETFs, and halal savings.
  • Careers and jobs: 16 career tracks that open once you finish The Earning Map (World 5).
  • And more: a market where players run stalls, homes, a gym, a masjid and a church, a conference centre with Web3 events.

What you leave with

  • A CV that fills in as you learn, at www.grinddistrict.app/cv/your-username.
  • Certificates for every lesson you pass, each with its own public link anyone can open.
  • Optional on-chain proof: mint a certificate as a soulbound NFT on Base or NEAR that stays with your account. How certificates work

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. It plays in your browser with nothing to download. Lessons, jobs and certificates are free.

Do I need a crypto wallet?

No. You can play and earn certificates without one. A wallet is only needed if you choose to mint a certificate on-chain.

I know nothing about crypto. Is it for me?

Yes. It is made for complete beginners. World 1 starts with how the internet and money work, and each lesson builds on the last.

Is the trading real money?

No. Everything on the trading floor uses in-game cash you earn by playing, never real money.

Does it work on my phone? In my language?

Yes. It runs in any modern phone browser (older phones get a lighter mode by themselves), and the game speaks 13 languages: English, Arabic, Chinese, Spanish, Hausa, Swahili, Japanese, Korean, Turkish, French, Portuguese, Hindi and Yoruba.
