Certificates: proof of what you learned

In Grind District, every lesson you pass becomes a certificate on your CV. Anyone can open its link to check it, and if you want it on-chain you can mint it as a soulbound NFT that stays with your account.

Earn your first certificate

What a certificate shows

The course you passed

Each of the 42 lessons on Campus has its own certificate, from how the internet works to wallets, blockchain basics and the ways people earn in Web3.

Who passed it, and when

Your name and username, the date, and a unique certificate ID.

A public link

Every certificate has its own page at www.grinddistrict.app/cert/ID. Share it on X, LinkedIn or in a DM, and whoever opens it sees exactly what you passed.

Your CV

Certificates collect on your CV at www.grinddistrict.app/cv/your-username, which you can share or download as a PDF from your Profile.

On-chain, if you want it

  • A short exam first. Minting needs a pass on a short certificate exam graded on the server, not just a pass saved on your phone.
  • Soulbound. The NFT cannot be sold or transferred: it proves what you learned, not what you bought.
  • Base or NEAR, your choice. It is minted to your own wallet: a Base wallet linked to your account, or your NEAR account.
  • Read back by the game. The certificate's public page checks the chain itself, never your device, and shows where it was minted. NEAR certificates are also marked Verified on your CV and nameplate in the game, and open the Holders' room in the Members lounge.

What it is, and what it is not

  • It is proof that you finished a specific lesson and passed its check, with a link anyone can verify.
  • It is not a university degree or a licence. It shows the steps you have taken, one lesson at a time.

How to get your first one

  • Open www.grinddistrict.app and tap Sign up.
  • Follow Zahra to Campus and pass the first lesson (about a minute).
  • Open Profile: your CV and your first certificate are there, ready to share.
