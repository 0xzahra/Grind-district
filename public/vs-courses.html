Best free ways to learn Web3 in 2026, and a game that gives you proof

There are good free ways to learn Web3. Most of them are built for people who want to write code. If you are starting from zero and want something to show for it, the right pick is different. Here is how three free options compare.

Who each one is for

CryptoZombies: you want to write smart contracts

Free, interactive Solidity lessons where you write code to build a game. Start here if you want to be a smart-contract developer.

LearnWeb3: you want structured developer courses

Free developer tracks and projects for building dapps. A good next step after CryptoZombies.

Grind District: you are starting from zero and want proof

A free 3D city in your browser. Take short lessons with a guide, work a shift at McGrind, practise trading with in-game cash (never real money), and every lesson you pass goes on your CV with a certificate anyone can check by link. Mint certificates as soulbound NFTs on Base or NEAR if you want on-chain proof. No coding, and no wallet needed to start.
